## Key Numbers — Verify in Every Image
- Correct purity = **99.99% (4N grade)** — NEVER 99.7%
- Diameter = **1.8mm or 2.0mm ± 0.05mm**
- Temper = **soft / fully annealed**
- Si limit = **≤ 30 ppm**
- Fe limit = **≤ 30 ppm**
- Cu limit = **≤ 10 ppm**
- Boat life with 99.7% wire = **80–120 hrs**
- Boat life with 99.99% wire = **160–220 hrs**
