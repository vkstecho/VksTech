Business, payment & enquiry information

Two distinct ways to work with VKS Tech. This page explains the purpose of each service and where payments are intended to be collected.

1. Industrial consulting and technology

Website: www.vkstech.com

VKS Tech provides manufacturing operations consulting, Excel and KPI automation, customized software and engineering tools. These commercial services are enquiry-based. No online payment gateway or payment links are integrated on this website for these services.

Request an introductory discussion. The scope, deliverables, timeline and commercial terms of any project are agreed separately before work begins. The free audit request does not itself confirm an appointment or create a paid order.

Request a project discussion →

2. Professional education, online mentoring and skill development

Website: mentor.vkstech.com

Mentor by VKS Tech is a separate online learning platform for students and working professionals. It offers learning sessions in AI, Excel, career skills, workplace productivity and flexible packaging technology, as well as related learning subscriptions.

Razorpay is intended exclusively for eligible paid learning sessions and educational subscriptions on Mentor, subject to merchant approval. It is not intended for industrial consulting, customized Excel automation, custom software projects or other commercial services. Review the current price, appointment availability and applicable service, subscription, cancellation and refund terms on Mentor before purchasing.

Visit Mentor →

3. Enquiry and privacy information

The consulting enquiry form asks for your name, company, contact details, selected service and optional problem description. These details are submitted to our site's Supabase-backed enquiry system so VKS Tech can respond. Newsletter registration is a separate action. Please do not submit confidential plant files, passwords, card details or OTPs through an enquiry.

The main site also uses browser storage for preferences and calculator state. Its visitor analytics uses a browser identifier, page path and referring URL to understand site usage. External services, including WhatsApp, YouTube, linked apps and the Mentor platform, have their own data handling arrangements.

For questions about your submitted information or a request to access or correct it, use the contact details below. For Mentor accounts or payment issues, contact the Mentor support team through the dedicated platform.
