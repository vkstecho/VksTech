👤 About Me

Vivek Kumar

I'm a Mechanical Engineer by training, a production leader by experience, and an app builder & mentor by choice. Over 9 years at UFlex Limited, JPFL Films (Jindal, Nashik) and GLS Polyfilms I led teams, trained and onboarded staff, and owned production planning, process control, quality and cost — delivering record output and near-zero critical defects.

Today I build practical apps for packaging professionals and offer one-to-one mentorship via Mentor App by VKS — covering AI tools, Excel, career skills, personal finance and related workplace skills.

Every calculator, every app, and every mentoring session is rooted in real plant-floor problems I solved myself. VKS Tech is the home for all of it.
