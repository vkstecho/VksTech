Refund and Cancellation Policy

VKS Tech is the legal seller for Mentor and an independent professional brand operated by an individual.

This policy covers eligible online mentoring, professional education, skill development sessions and educational learning subscriptions.

When a refund is available

  • If the Mentor cannot deliver a booked session, we will offer a full refund or reschedule the session with your consent.
  • Verified duplicate payments are refundable.
  • Successful payments for services that were not delivered are eligible for a refund after verification.
  • Successfully completed sessions are generally non-refundable, subject to valid service complaints and applicable consumer rights.

Refund requests should normally be submitted within seven days of purchase. The eligibility conditions above and your statutory consumer rights apply.

How to request a refund

Contact VKS Tech support with the booking or payment reference and a brief explanation. Use Payments & receipts to find the reference and view the payment status. Keep card details, payment PINs and one-time codes private.

VKS Tech verifies each request. You can check Payments & receipts for recorded refund status or contact support for an update.

Cancelling or changing a session

Open My Sessions to review your appointment and respond to a proposed time change. To request cancellation or a different time, contact support or use your booking chat when available. Include the booking reference and appointment time.

Subscription renewal and access

To stop future renewals, select Cancel renewal in your Mentor account. Your access continues until the end of the current paid period. For a question about an existing payment, contact support with the reference.
