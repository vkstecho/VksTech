📌 Formula
GSM = Micron × Density (g/cm³)
Micron = GSM ÷ Density

When you need this

  • Supplier quote is in μm; your BOM is in GSM
  • Lab coupon weight → GSM; machine setting is in micron
  • Comparing PET vs BOPP at the “same” thickness (weight is not the same)

Step-by-step

Example A — Micron → GSM: PET 12 μm

  1. Open vkstech.com/calculators/gsm-to-micron
  2. Choose tab Micron → GSM
  3. Film type PET (1.38), thickness 12
  4. Result: 16.56 GSM

Example B — GSM → Micron: BOPP 18 GSM

  1. Switch to GSM → Micron
  2. Film type BOPP (0.91), GSM 18
  3. Result: ~19.8 μm

Density cheat sheet

  • PET 1.38 · BOPP 0.91 · CPP 0.90 · LDPE 0.92 · Paper 0.80

Same micron, different polymer → different GSM. That is why 12 μm PET is heavier than 12 μm BOPP.

Common mistakes

  • Using PET density on a BOPP job
  • Confusing g/cm³ density with kg/m³ (the tool uses the correct scale for each mode)
  • Rounding too early when quoting customers
