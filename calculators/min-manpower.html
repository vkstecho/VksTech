Formula

Relief = Roster days ÷ (Roster days − weekly offs). Example: 7 ÷ 6 ≈ 1.167.

Process headcount = ceil( On-duty machine crew × Shifts × Relief × (1 + Buffer%) )

Shared headcount = ceil( (Eng + Sup + Other) × Relief × (1 + Buffer%) )

Total = Process + Shared
