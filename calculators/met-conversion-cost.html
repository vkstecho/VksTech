Met Conversion Cost

Turn shift or order costs into ₹/kg conversion cost, and optionally compare with a target or rate-card equivalent.

Actual ₹/kg = Total conversion cost ÷ Output kg

What to include in “conversion cost”

  • Aluminium / boat / energy / labour allocated to the run
  • Or full shift conversion pool if you are doing daily review

If you enter a target ₹/kg, the tool shows variance. If you enter base film cost, it also shows landed ₹/kg (base + conversion).
