Cost per Square Meter

Convert material cost in ₹/kg into ₹ per square meter using GSM. Useful for quotes, laminate costing and customer comparisons.

₹/sqm = (₹/kg × GSM) ÷ 1000

Why this matters

Customers and estimators often think in area. Same ₹/kg looks very different at 12 GSM vs 40 GSM.

  • 1 kg of 10 GSM film covers 100 sqm → ₹/sqm = rate ÷ 100
  • 1 kg of 20 GSM film covers 50 sqm → ₹/sqm = rate ÷ 50
