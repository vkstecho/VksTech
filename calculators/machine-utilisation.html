Machine Utilisation %

See what share of available shift time the machine was actually running. Complements yield and capacity planning.

Utilisation % = (Running time ÷ Available shift time) × 100
—
Fill running & available time
% util

Notes

  • Available time = shift length minus planned breaks (or full shift if you include breaks in downtime)
  • Running time = time the machine was producing (exclude setup, breakdown, no-plan, quality holds if you want pure run util)
  • This is not full OEE — OEE also multiplies availability × performance × quality

Low utilisation with high yield usually means planning or material gaps. High utilisation with low yield means process/quality issues.
