How the formula works

Volume of film = length × width × thickness. Convert units so everything is in metres, then multiply by density (kg/m³) to get mass in kg.

  • Width is entered in mm → divide by 1000 to get metres.
  • Thickness is entered in micron (μm) → divide by 1,000,000 to get metres.
  • Density is material-specific (PET denser than BOPP).

Example: 10,000 m × 1500 mm × 12 μm PET → about 248.4 kg.
