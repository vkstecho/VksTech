Roll Outer Diameter

Estimate finished roll OD for storage racks, forklift handling and machine max-OD limits. Uses the standard film-winding area formula.

OD (mm) = √( Core ID² + (4 × Length_m × Thickness_μm) / π )

Common cores: 76 mm (3″) and 152 mm (6″) in flexible packaging.
