    Renewal and cancellation

    A verified purchase activates or extends access for the period shown on the plan. Renewals are made through a new checkout. To stop renewal, sign in and choose Payments & receipts → Learning subscriptions → Cancel renewal. Cancelling renewal stops future renewals and keeps access through the end of the current paid period.

    How we deliver your session

    1. Choose your topic, session duration and an available appointment time. Times are shown in IST.
    2. Review the amount and complete Razorpay Checkout. After payment is verified, the reserved time awaits Mentor confirmation.
    3. Open My Sessions to check confirmation, chat with the Mentor and join once the meeting link is available.

    Sessions and subscription content are delivered online. Where a session recording is made available, you can find it in your workspace.
