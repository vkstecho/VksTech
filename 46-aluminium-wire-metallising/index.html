🔬 Purity — The Parameter That Matters Most

Aluminium purity is expressed as a percentage — the fraction of the metal that is actually aluminium, with the remainder being trace impurities. For vacuum metallising in flexible packaging, purity is not just a quality preference — it is a technical requirement driven by what happens to those impurities inside the vacuum chamber at 1400°C.

Three purity grades — what each contains, where each is used, and the real cost of choosing the wrong one for your application.

The main impurities in commercial aluminium are silicon (Si), iron (Fe), and copper (Cu). At room temperature these are locked in the aluminium lattice and do nothing. But in a diffusion pump-evacuated vacuum chamber at 1400°C, these impurities behave very differently from aluminium:

Silicon (Si) — vaporises at a slightly different rate than aluminium. Silicon deposits on the film as SiO₂ when it reacts with residual oxygen in the chamber. SiO₂ particles are hard, transparent, and create micro-defects in the coating — visible as haze, adhesion spots, or pinholes under magnification. Silicon also reacts with the BN phase of the evaporation boat ceramic, accelerating surface erosion and reducing boat life significantly.

Iron (Fe) — has a much higher boiling point than aluminium (~2862°C vs ~2470°C). Iron tends to accumulate in the molten aluminium pool in the boat cavity rather than evaporate cleanly. Over time it builds up as a dark sludge-like deposit, reduces the effective wetting area, and causes uneven evaporation and hot spots on the boat surface.

Copper (Cu) — evaporates readily and deposits with the aluminium on the film. Copper contamination in a food packaging coating can be a regulatory concern. More practically, copper changes the optical properties of the coating — the film develops a slight warm or brownish tint instead of the clean silver-white of pure aluminium, which shows up under the spectrometer and in OD measurements.

📏 Diameter — Consistency Matters as Much as Size

The diameter of the aluminium wire determines how much aluminium is delivered to the boat per unit length of wire fed. Your metalliser's wire feed mechanism — typically a set of driven rollers — is calibrated to deliver a specific mass of aluminium per minute based on the wire diameter it expects. If the actual wire diameter is wrong, or inconsistent along the length, the feed rate is effectively wrong, and coating thickness becomes uneven.

The standard diameters for flexible packaging metallising in India are 1.8mm and 2.0mm. Some machines are designed for 1.5mm. The right diameter for your machine is specified in your machine manual — confirm with your machine manufacturer or maintenance team if unclear.

What matters equally is the diameter tolerance. A wire specified at 2.0mm that actually measures 1.95mm in one section and 2.05mm in another will cause variation in wire feed rate — which translates directly to OD variation across the film and between rolls. The tolerance should be specified as ± 0.05mm maximum. Ask for this in your purchase order and insist on a dimensional report from the supplier.

A related issue is ovality — where the wire cross-section is not perfectly circular but slightly elliptical. Oval wire does not feed consistently through round drive rollers, causes intermittent slippage, and creates coating streaks. Any reputable supplier will confirm a roundness specification alongside the diameter tolerance.

🔧 Temper — Why Soft Wire is Non-Negotiable

Temper refers to the hardness state of the wire — how stiff or soft it is. Aluminium wire can be supplied in three temper states: hard-drawn (stiff, maximum strength), half-hard (intermediate), and soft/fully annealed (ductile, minimum stiffness). For vacuum metallising, soft/fully annealed is the only acceptable temper.

Hard-drawn wire resists bending, causes feed mechanism jams, kinks, and inconsistent delivery. Soft/annealed wire feeds smoothly, consistently, and without resistance through every curve in the feed path.

Here is why temper matters so practically. The wire feed path in a metalliser starts from a spool, travels through guide tubes and rollers, sometimes around bends, before reaching the boat. Soft annealed wire is ductile — it bends easily around every curve in this path without resistance, feeds at a perfectly consistent rate through the driven rollers, and arrives at the boat at exactly the rate commanded by the machine controller.

Hard-drawn wire has internal stress from the drawing process. It resists bending — if it has been wound on a spool and then straightens itself, it can push back against the feed rollers, causing inconsistent feed rate. Worse, if there is a kink in hard-drawn wire (from spool storage, shipping, or handling), it can jam in the feed tube, cause a sudden feed interruption, and leave the boat running at full power with no wire — the fastest way to puncture a boat (described in Blog #45). Hard-drawn wire can also snap in the feed mechanism at wire joins.

When you receive a new batch of wire, a simple quality check is to pull a short length from the spool and let it hang freely. Soft annealed wire will fall straight and curve gently under its own weight. Hard wire will spring back and try to hold its coiled shape. If the wire springs back noticeably, it is not properly annealed.

✨ Surface Condition — The One Nobody Checks Until It Is Too Late

The surface of metallising aluminium wire should be bright, clean, and oil-free. This sounds obvious but is one of the most common sources of contamination problems in Indian plants.

During wire drawing, lubricants are used to reduce friction between the wire and the drawing dies. These lubricants must be completely removed before the wire is suitable for vacuum metallising. If residual drawing oil remains on the wire surface and enters the vacuum chamber, it vaporises and deposits as a hydrocarbon contamination layer on the chamber walls, the film, and the boat surface. This contamination:

Reduces the adhesion of printing inks and adhesives to the metallised film. Increases outgassing in the vacuum chamber — the system takes longer to reach process vacuum between runs. Deposits a thin organic film on the boat surface that interferes with aluminium wetting. Over time, accumulates on chamber walls and requires a full chamber clean to remove.

Some suppliers coat wire with a light mineral oil to prevent surface oxidation during storage and shipping. This is acceptable only if your plant has a wire degreasing or cleaning step before the wire enters the chamber. Most Indian flexible packaging plants do not have this — so oil-free wire is essential. Always ask your supplier explicitly: is this wire supplied oil-free for direct use in vacuum metallising?

The surface should also be free from oxide scale. A small amount of natural aluminium oxide (Al₂O₃) on the surface is unavoidable — aluminium oxidises immediately on contact with air and this thin native oxide is harmless. Heavy oxide scale (dark grey patches, rough surface) is a sign of poor storage or old stock. It generates oxide particles in the boat that create sludge and coating defects.

🧵 Spool — The Packaging That Affects Production

The way the wire is wound onto the spool directly affects how it feeds in production. A well-wound spool feeds consistently and runs to the end without tangles. A poorly wound spool causes feed problems, kinks, and wasted wire at the end of the spool.

Level winding is the correct winding method — wire is laid in neat parallel rows, each row completely filled before the next begins, like thread on a sewing bobbin. Level-wound spools feed consistently and predictably. Random winding — where wire is loosely wound in an irregular pile — causes the wire to cross and tangle, leading to sudden feed resistance, jerky delivery, and coating streaks.

Standard spool sizes for flexible packaging metallising in India are 5 kg and 10 kg spools on plastic or aluminium cores. Heavier spools (20 kg) are available but require proper spool-holder support in the machine. The spool core diameter must match your machine's spool holder spindle — confirm this before ordering from a new supplier.

📋 How to Read a Certificate of Analysis

Every batch of 99.99% aluminium wire should come with a Certificate of Analysis (CoA) from the manufacturer. This document lists the actual measured composition of the batch. Here is how to read it:

Aluminium (Al): Should read 99.99% minimum — or expressed as ≥ 99.99. If it reads 99.98% or below, the wire does not meet the 4N specification. Reject the batch.

Silicon (Si): The most damaging impurity for metallising. Should be ≤ 30 ppm (0.003%) in a properly controlled 4N wire. Values above 50 ppm will start to cause visible coating quality issues.

Iron (Fe): Should be ≤ 30 ppm. Higher iron causes boat sludge build-up and OD unevenness.

Copper (Cu): Should be ≤ 10 ppm for food packaging. Copper is a food safety concern at higher levels and changes coating optical properties.

Other elements (Mn, Zn, Ti, etc.): Each should be in single-digit ppm range. Their combined total determines the actual purity level.

A CoA that shows only "Al ≥ 99.99%" without individual impurity values is not adequate. A proper CoA will list each trace element individually with its measured value in ppm. If your current supplier cannot provide individual element analysis, that is a red flag.

🔬 Purity — वो Parameter जो सबसे ज़्यादा Matter करता है

Aluminium purity percentage में express होती है — metal का वो fraction जो actually aluminium है, बाकी trace impurities। Vacuum metallising के लिए purity सिर्फ़ quality preference नहीं है — ये एक technical requirement है जो इस बात से drive होती है कि वो impurities 1400°C पर vacuum chamber के अंदर क्या करती हैं।

तीन purity grades — हर एक में क्या होता है, कहाँ use होता है, और wrong choice का real cost।

Commercial aluminium में main impurities silicon (Si), iron (Fe), और copper (Cu) हैं। Room temperature पर ये aluminium lattice में locked हैं और कुछ नहीं करतीं। लेकिन 1400°C पर evacuated vacuum chamber में ये aluminium से बहुत differently behave करती हैं:

Silicon (Si) — aluminium से slightly different rate पर vaporise होता है। Chamber में residual oxygen से react करके SiO₂ deposit करता है — film पर hard, transparent micro-defects create करता है। Boat ceramic के BN phase के साथ भी react करता है — boat life significantly कम करता है।

Iron (Fe) — aluminium से बहुत higher boiling point (~2862°C vs ~2470°C)। Boat cavity में molten aluminium pool में cleanly evaporate होने की बजाय accumulate होता है — dark sludge-like deposit बनाता है, effective wetting area reduce करता है, uneven evaporation और hot spots cause करता है।

Copper (Cu) — readily evaporate होता है और aluminium के साथ film पर deposit होता है। Food packaging coating में copper contamination regulatory concern हो सकती है। Practically, copper coating के optical properties change करता है — film में clean silver-white की बजाय slight warm या brownish tint आती है।

📏 Diameter — Size जितना Consistency Matter करती है

Aluminium wire का diameter determine करता है कि wire की per unit length कितना aluminium boat को deliver होता है। अगर actual wire diameter wrong है, या along the length inconsistent है, तो feed rate effectively wrong है — coating thickness uneven हो जाती है।

India में flexible packaging metallising के लिए standard diameters 1.8mm और 2.0mm हैं। आपकी machine के लिए right diameter machine manual में specified है।

Diameter tolerance उतनी ही important है। 2.0mm specified wire जो actually एक section में 1.95mm और दूसरे में 2.05mm measure करे — wire feed rate में variation cause करेगी जो directly OD variation में translate होती है। Tolerance ± 0.05mm maximum specify करनी चाहिए।

Ovality भी एक issue है — जहाँ wire cross-section perfectly circular नहीं बल्कि slightly elliptical है। Oval wire round drive rollers में consistently feed नहीं होती — intermittent slippage, jerky delivery, और coating streaks।

🔧 Temper — Soft Wire Non-Negotiable क्यों है

Temper wire की hardness state है — कितनी stiff या soft है। Aluminium wire तीन temper states में supply हो सकती है: hard-drawn (stiff), half-hard (intermediate), और soft/fully annealed (ductile)। Vacuum metallising के लिए soft/fully annealed ही एकमात्र acceptable temper है।

Hard-drawn wire bending resist करती है, feed mechanism jams, kinks, और inconsistent delivery cause करती है। Soft/annealed wire feed path के हर curve में smoothly, consistently, और बिना resistance के feed होती है।

Hard-drawn wire में drawing process से internal stress होता है। ये bending resist करती है — अगर spool पर wound है और खुद straight होने की कोशिश करती है, feed rollers के against push back कर सकती है — inconsistent feed rate। Worse, अगर hard-drawn wire में kink हो, feed tube में jam हो सकती है — sudden feed interruption — और boat full power पर बिना wire के run करे — puncture का fastest way (Blog #45 में described)।

एक simple quality check: spool से short length pull करें और freely hang होने दें। Soft annealed wire straight fall होगी अपने weight के नीचे। Hard wire spring back करेगी और coiled shape hold करने की कोशिश करेगी।

✨ Surface Condition — जो कोई Check नहीं करता जब तक देर न हो जाए

Metallising aluminium wire की surface bright, clean, और oil-free होनी चाहिए।

Wire drawing के दौरान lubricants use होते हैं die और wire के बीच friction reduce करने के लिए। ये lubricants vacuum metallising से पहले completely remove होने चाहिए। अगर residual drawing oil wire surface पर रहे और vacuum chamber में enter करे, ये hydrocarbon contamination layer के रूप में vaporise और deposit होती है। ये contamination:

Metallised film पर printing inks और adhesives की adhesion कम करती है। Vacuum chamber में outgassing बढ़ाती है — system runs के बीच process vacuum तक पहुँचने में ज़्यादा time लेता है। Boat surface पर एक thin organic film deposit करती है जो aluminium wetting interfere करती है।

कुछ suppliers wire को storage के दौरान surface oxidation prevent करने के लिए light mineral oil से coat करते हैं — यह acceptable है सिर्फ़ तब जब plant में wire degreasing stage हो। ज़्यादातर Indian flexible packaging plants में ये नहीं होता — इसलिए oil-free wire essential है।

📋 Certificate of Analysis कैसे पढ़ें

99.99% aluminium wire के हर batch के साथ manufacturer का Certificate of Analysis (CoA) होना चाहिए। ये document batch की actual measured composition list करता है। इसे कैसे read करें:

Aluminium (Al): 99.99% minimum पढ़ना चाहिए। अगर 99.98% या कम read करे — batch 4N specification नहीं meet करता। Reject करें।

Silicon (Si): सबसे damaging impurity। ≤ 30 ppm होना चाहिए properly controlled 4N wire में। 50 ppm से ऊपर values visible coating quality issues cause करेंगी।

Iron (Fe): ≤ 30 ppm होना चाहिए। Higher iron boat sludge build-up और OD unevenness cause करता है।

Copper (Cu): Food packaging के लिए ≤ 10 ppm। Copper food safety concern है और coating optical properties change करता है।

एक CoA जो सिर्फ़ "Al ≥ 99.99%" show करे बिना individual impurity values के — adequate नहीं है। Proper CoA में हर trace element individually ppm में list होगा। अगर current supplier individual element analysis provide नहीं कर सकता — ये red flag है।

CoA पर क्या देखें — चार critical impurities, acceptable limits, और red flags जो batch reject करने या supplier change करने का signal हैं।

Complete desk reference — purity grades, specification checklist, CoA reading guide, और cheap wire actually कितना costly है। A4 print करें, laminate करें, purchase team के पास रखें। Excel formats नीचे।

💡 Key Takeaway: Vacuum metallising के लिए aluminium wire commodity नहीं है — ये चार critical parameters वाला precision consumable है: purity (99.99% minimum, 4N grade — कभी 99.7% नहीं), diameter (1.8mm या 2.0mm ± 0.05mm, पूरे spool में consistent), temper (soft/fully annealed — hard-drawn कभी नहीं), और surface condition (bright, clean, oil-free)। तीन main impurities — silicon, iron, और copper — 1400°C पर aluminium से differently behave करती हैं: silicon coating defects create करता है और boat ceramic corrode करता है; iron boat sludge के रूप में build up होता है; copper coating को optically और chemically contaminate करता है। हर batch के साथ full CoA होनी चाहिए जो individual trace element values ppm में list करे। 99.7% wire पर saving सिर्फ़ increased boat consumption से दो से तीन गुना wipe out होती है। हर purchase order पर full wire standard specify करें — purity, diameter tolerance, temper, surface condition, और individual element limits।
