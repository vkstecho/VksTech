Privacy Policy

VKS Tech is the legal seller for Mentor and an independent professional brand operated by an individual.

This policy explains how VKS Tech handles information when you use Mentor’s website, installed web app or related Android app.

Information we collect

  • Phone number — for login via OTP (Firebase Phone Authentication).
  • Account, session and learning data includes profile details you provide, appointment and learning activity, booking or topic chat, staff notes, and recordings where made available.
  • Payment records include amounts, transaction identifiers, verification and refund status, subscription history, and references you provide for historical payments or support.
  • Technical data — basic device/browser info, IP (via hosting/Firebase logs), and install status if you use the PWA or Android app.
  • Optional contact info — if you email or message us (name, email, WhatsApp).

We do not sell your personal data. We do not require access to your contacts, camera, or files unless you choose to upload something yourself.

How we use information

  • To create and secure your Mentor account (OTP login).
  • To deliver mentorship features: catalogue, bookings, recordings, doubt chat, and admin tools for the mentor.
  • To verify Razorpay payments, review historical claims and refunds, and provide eligible session or subscription access.
  • To improve reliability, prevent abuse, and respond to support requests.

Razorpay Checkout

Razorpay handles payment instrument details for eligible online mentoring, professional education, skill development sessions and educational learning subscriptions. Mentor records order and payment references and the data needed to verify the transaction, issue receipts and track refunds. Checkout may receive your registered mobile number as a contact.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase for login, database and storage.
  • Razorpay for eligible mentoring and learning payments.
  • Hosting and delivery providers for our website, plus the meeting provider or WhatsApp when you choose to use those links.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies and applicable law.

Data storage and security

We use HTTPS and access controls to restrict private Mentor records to the relevant account and authorized Mentor staff. Browser storage supports preferences, sign-in and remembered-device features.

Data retention

We keep account and mentorship data while your account is active and as needed for mentorship, legal, or dispute purposes. You may request deletion (see below).

Requests about your data

Subject to applicable law, you may request:

  • Access to data we hold about you
  • Correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion of your account and related data

Email Care@vkstech.com and identify your Mentor account. Support may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Children

Mentor is aimed at adults and working professionals. It is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where required). We do not knowingly collect data from children.
