💧 Film and finished rolls are sensitive to humidity and temperature. Poor storage creates blocking, dimensional change and later process problems.

📖 9–11 min read · Practical plant guide · Production + Quality + PPC

This topic is one of the quiet levers that separates stable converting plants from those that constantly fight the same problems. The article below gives the practical view used on the floor and in planning meetings.

📌 Key Takeaways
  • Define the critical parameter and the acceptable range
  • Measure or observe it at the right frequency
  • Log results so trends and ownership are visible
  • Link the parameter to the defects or cost it drives
  • Review and act — measurement without action is decoration

Why This Matters on the Floor

When the parameter is controlled, waste drops, claims reduce and capacity becomes more predictable. When it is ignored, the same defects and arguments return every week. Make the control visible in the daily routine and in the shift handover.

💡 Floor Rule: If you cannot show the last measurement or the last review of this parameter, the process is running on hope. Hope is not a control method.
