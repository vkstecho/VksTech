💧 Film Stored Open in Monsoon or Next to a Hot Dryer Does Not “Become Bad Overnight” — It Follows Physics You Can Plan For.

📖 9-min read · Storage · Quality

Wrap integrity, FIFO, distance from heat sources, and humidity for moisture-sensitive structures (including some EVOH builds). Ageing printed/laminated WIP has rules too.

📌 Storage Basics
  • Keep wraps closed until use
  • FIFO with visible dates
  • Away from dryer exhaust and direct sun
  • Special care for barrier-sensitive structures
💡 PPC: Aged WIP on the floor is a planning failure, not only a warehouse issue.
