Logic used:
1. Available minutes per day (after uptime) → theoretical meters = Speed × available minutes
2. Number of Changeovers = Theoretical meters ÷ Roll Length (24,000 m default)
3. Changeover Loss % = (Changeovers × Changeover Time) ÷ Available minutes × 100
4. Final efficiency for that process = Uptime × (1 – Changeover Loss) × (1 – Quality) × Speed Eff
5. Capacity (MT) is then calculated using the process-specific efficiency.
Typical values: Roll length 20,000–30,000 m | Changeover 15–40 min | Overall efficiency after all losses usually 55–75% in converting plants.
