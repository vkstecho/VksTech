## Quality bar (every blog)

1. **Plant-first** — process engineers, supervisors, plant heads in Indian flexible packaging  
2. **Numbers** — real ranges / formulas / before-after (no fake precision)  
3. **Actionable** — checklist, decision tree, or next-shift discipline  
4. **Bilingual** — full EN + HI in one `index.html`  
5. **No collision** — check this file; if close to an old post, state the *new angle* in Notes  
6. **Assets** — 3 cover variants  
7. **Meta** — valid category (+ subcategory only for packaging)
