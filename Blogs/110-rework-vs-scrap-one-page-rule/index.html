⚖️ Rework vs Scrap: A One-Page Decision Rule All Shifts Use

📖 8-min read  |  Plant Operations  |  Practical, numbers-first

When a roll is doubtful, plants lose in two ways: scrap too fast (money in the bin) or hope too long (silent piles, late surprises, customer risk).

Day shift “knows” the unwritten rule. Night shift guesses or waits until morning. That is not a quality system — it is a shift-dependent habit.

💡 Simple test: Would night shift make the same rework/scrap call as your best day-shift lead — using only a posted page? If not, the rule still lives in heads.

One-Page Decision (Keep It Short)

  1. Safety or contamination risk? → Scrap. No debate on the floor.
  2. Customer spec clearly failed and not recoverable? → Scrap or formal QA hold per your procedure.
  3. Can it meet spec with a defined rework? (trim, rewind, sort, reclaim path you already allow) → Rework with owner and by when.
  4. Unsure? → Hold is allowed — but hold needs a name, a reason, and a decision deadline. Not a silent corner forever.

Post this at the hold area and QA desk. Film-specific limits (what rework is allowed) can sit as a second line on the job family recipe (Blog 104) when needed.

What Every Hold Must Show

  • Why it is held (one line)
  • Decision: rework / scrap / waiting QA
  • Owner initials
  • Time in / decision-by time

If any field is blank, it is not a hold — it is a lost roll.

2-Week Rule Discipline

  • One-page rule posted where holds actually sit
  • Night shift uses the same page — no “ask tomorrow” for routine calls
  • Daily meeting: list holds older than your agreed hours (e.g. 12 or 24)
  • Week 2 target: no unnamed piles; repeating defect types feed the waste board (Blog 108)

Link to Waste Board and First Good

Waste board (108) — Scrap and rework both need a reason bucket; “other” should shrink.

First good (103) — Clear rules reduce argument over borderline startup metres.

Customer complaints (72) — Inconsistent “ship it” calls on night shift are a common root.

Key Takeaway

Write a one-page rework vs scrap rule: safety, clear fail, defined rework with owner, or timed hold. Same page on every shift. Empty holds are not holds. Consistent calls protect both the bin and the customer.

⚖️ रीवर्क vs स्क्रैप: एक-पेज नियम जो हर शिफ्ट इस्तेमाल करे

📖 9-min read  |  प्लांट ऑपरेशन्स

जब रोल संदेहास्पद हो, प्लांट दो तरह से हारता है: बहुत जल्दी स्क्रैप या बहुत देर तक आशा (खामोश ढेर, देर से सरप्राइज, ग्राहक जोखिम)।

💡 सरल टेस्ट: क्या नाइट शिफ्ट केवल पोस्टेड पेज से वैसा ही रीवर्क/स्क्रैप कॉल करेगी जैसा दिन का सबसे अच्छा लीड? अगर नहीं, नियम अभी दिमाग में है।

एक-पेज निर्णय

  1. सेफ्टी / संदूषण जोखिम? → स्क्रैप
  2. कस्टमर स्पेक साफ फेल और रिकवर नहीं? → स्क्रैप या औपचारिक होल्ड
  3. परिभाषित रीवर्क से स्पेक संभव? → रीवर्क + ओनर + कब तक
  4. अनिश्चित? → होल्ड — नाम, वजह, निर्णय की समय सीमा के साथ

मुख्य निष्कर्ष

एक-पेज रीवर्क vs स्क्रैप नियम लिखें। हर शिफ्ट एक पेज। खाली होल्ड होल्ड नहीं। एक जैसे कॉल बिन और ग्राहक दोनों बचाते हैं।
