## 🌟 IMAGE 2: MEETING STRUCTURE — `meeting-structure.jpg`

**Dimensions:** 1400 × 900

```
Clean modern infographic on soft cream background.

Title (navy, bold): "The 15-Minute Structure"

Five horizontal timed blocks (like a simple timeline or stacked cards):

1. 0–2 min  — Attendance & Safety (who is missing, any incident)
2. 2–5 min  — Yesterday’s numbers (output, waste, major stops only)
3. 5–9 min  — Today’s risks (material, machine, skill coverage)
4. 9–13 min — Decisions & owners (only what changes today)
5. 13–15 min — Confirm & close (one sentence each owner)

Footer: "If it needs more than 15 minutes, the data is not ready — fix the data, not the meeting length."
```

## 🌟 IMAGE 3: WEAK vs STRONG MEETING — `weak-vs-strong.jpg`

**Dimensions:** 1400 × 900

```
Side-by-side comparison, clean infographic style.

LEFT (warning tone) — "WEAK MEETING"
- Long discussion, no numbers
- Everyone talks, no owner
- Same problems repeated daily
- Ends with "we will see"
- 25–40 minutes, nothing changes on the floor

RIGHT (success tone) — "STRONG 15-MIN MEETING"
- Only 4–5 numbers on the board
- Risks named with owners
- Decisions written before leaving
- Ends on time
- Shift starts with clarity

Bottom orange banner:
"The meeting is not the work. The meeting is to make the work clearer."
```
