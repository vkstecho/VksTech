📏 How to Use the Roll Length from Weight Calculator — Live Walkthrough

📖 3-min read  |  Tool guide + video  |  Metres left when you only know kg

You know the roll weight, width and micron — but not the remaining length. Dispatch, splicing or inventory often need metres. Use the free Roll Length from Weight calculator (reverse of the roll weight formula).

▶ Watch on YouTube Shorts

📌 Formula
L (m) = Weight (kg) ÷ (Width mm/1000 × Thickness μm/10⁶ × Density)

When you need this

  • Part-used roll — estimate metres left from current weight
  • Stock system has kg; planner needs metres for the job
  • Cross-check a supplier length claim against weighed kg

Step-by-step example

PET roll, 25 kg, 1500 mm wide, 12 micron.

  1. Open vkstech.com/calculators/roll-length
  2. Film type PET
  3. Weight 25 · Width 1500 · Thickness 12
  4. Result: length in metres (rounded)

Common mistakes

  • Wrong film density (PET vs BOPP)
  • Width entered in metres instead of mm
  • Using net film weight but including core weight in the kg figure
