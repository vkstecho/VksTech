# Product Improvement Suggestions — From Blog #91 Research

These suggestions come directly from the operator-shortage problem and how plants would actually use the apps.

## Man Power (Highest Priority)

1. **Skill Matrix / Competency Tracker** (Strongly recommended)
   - Add ability to define critical skills per machine/section (e.g. Metalliser Boat Change, Vacuum Recovery, Wire Alignment, Source Cleaning).
   - Each operator gets status: Learning / Practising / Competent / Can Train.
   - Show skill coverage % on the schedule view.
   - Auto-schedule can prefer “Competent” operators on critical machines.

2. **Training Assignment + Completion Tracking**
   - Ability to assign free Mentor modules (Met Train Pro etc.) to specific operators.
   - Simple mark-as-complete or quiz pass/fail.
   - Visible progress on team page.

3. **Digital Process Checklists**
   - Boat-change checklist and Shift-handover checklist that operators tick on mobile.
   - Timestamped + optional photo.
   - Manager can see completion rate.

4. **Knowledge-loss Alert**
   - When a “Competent” or “Can Train” operator goes on long leave or is marked Left, surface a warning and list the skills that are now under-covered.

## Mentor App

1. **Learning Paths**
   - Pre-built paths: “New Metallizer Operator”, “Shift In-charge”, “Process Engineer”.
   - Free modules sequenced with suggested order.

2. **Completion Certificates**
   - Simple digital certificate when a free module or path is finished (motivates operators and gives managers evidence).

3. **Tighter Man Power Bridge**
   - From Man Power training section, one-click open of the relevant Mentor free module.

## PackLink

1. **Skill-tagged Job Posts**
   - Allow “Metallizer Operator – Competent in boat change & vacuum” type filters.
   - Companies can signal “urgently need trained metallizer operators”.

2. **Internal Mobility Signal**
   - Option for members to mark “Open to internal training / cross-plant movement”.

---

These upgrades would make the blog’s “Practical Fix Stack” even more powerful and increase stickiness of all three apps.
