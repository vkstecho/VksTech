## SEQUENCE RULES (1400×900)
```
Cream infographic title: "Sequence Rules That Save Minutes"
Five rules as cards:
1. Same structure family together
2. Light → dark (or plant ink rule) when print matters
3. Width changes batched, not random
4. Hard tooling changes grouped at shift edges when possible
5. Urgent job inserted with eyes open — cost is known
Bottom: "Every extra changeover is a choice — sometimes required, never invisible."
```
