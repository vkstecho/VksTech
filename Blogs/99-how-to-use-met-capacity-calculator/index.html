⚙️ How to Use the Metalliser Capacity Calculator — Live Walkthrough

📖 5-min read  |  Tool guide + video  |  MT/day for a real product mix

“What is our Met capacity?” is never one number. It changes with width, micron, speed, jumbo length and boat-set life. This guide walks through the free Met Capacity calculator so you can answer for a specific mix — not a ratecard average.

📌 3-step engine
  1. Rolls/day from cycle time + cleaning overhead
  2. Roll weight from width × length × micron × density
  3. Capacity (MT/day) = rolls/day × roll kg ÷ 1000

When you need this

  • Monthly capacity plan for a known product mix
  • Comparing two jumbos or two boat-set lives
  • Answering “can we take this order this week?”

Step-by-step (typical PET day)

  1. Open vkstech.com/calculators/met-capacity
  2. Film: PET · Width: 3000 mm · Micron: 12
  3. Cycle: Setup 20 · Vacuum+Heat 20 · Vent 10 · Speed 800 · Jumbo 54000 · Boat change 10
  4. Boat-set life: 600000 m · Cleaning/day: 20 min
  5. Read MT/day and the breakdown (rolls/day × kg/roll · cycle minutes)

Change only micron or speed and watch capacity move — that is the point of the tool.

What drives the number most

  • Run time = jumbo length ÷ line speed
  • Boat overhead grows when boat-set life is short
  • Cleaning minutes reduce available minutes in the day

Common mistakes

  • Using one “nameplate” capacity for every product
  • Ignoring boat-change time on short boat life
  • Forgetting source cleaning in the available minutes
