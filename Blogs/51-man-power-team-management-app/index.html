1. What Man Power Actually Does

Man Power is a PWA (works on phone and desktop) focused on workforce coordination for packaging plants:

  • Team upload & structure — Import members via Excel with Section / Machine / Responsibility. Managers see their own people; admins see the full plant.
  • Shift scheduling — Build and publish shift plans with smart filters. Team members open “My Shift” and instantly know where they are posted.
  • Leave & attendance flow — Leave requests move through approval instead of scattered WhatsApp messages. Pending items stay visible until closed.
  • Reports — Quick views for supervisors and plant heads without rebuilding Excel every week.
  • To-Do & instructions — Capture open tasks and department instructions so knowledge does not live only in one person’s head.
  • Training / Learn modules — Space for skill development and process notes (including links to related cost tools where configured).

Everything is designed for the shop-floor reality: intermittent connectivity, Hindi/English users, and managers who need answers in under a minute.

2. How to Start Using It (Step-by-Step)

  1. Open the app — Go to manpower.vkstech.com on your phone or browser. Add to Home Screen for a native-like experience.
  2. Login with phone OTP — Managers and team members both use phone number + OTP. No complex passwords to forget.
  3. Admin / Manager approval — New registrations are approved by admin or manager (this also reduces unnecessary OTP cost). Once approved, the member appears in the team list.
  4. Upload or add team — Use Excel import (Section / Machine / Responsibility columns) or add people one by one.
  5. Publish the shift — Create the roster for the coming days. Filters help when you manage multiple machines or sections.
  6. Team uses “My Shift” — Operators open the same app and see only their own posting. No more asking “Where am I today?”
  7. Leave & pending — Requests appear in Pending Approvals. Approve or reject in the app; status is visible to the requester.
  8. Reports & instructions — Use Reports for monthly review and Instructions for standing department notes.

First-time setup for a typical metalliser department usually takes under an hour once the Excel team list is ready.

3. How It Saves Time (Real Plant Benefits)

Supervisors and shift in-charges lose time in three places: (1) building and rewriting shift lists, (2) chasing leave and attendance on chat, (3) answering the same “where am I posted?” questions every day. Man Power attacks all three:

  • One source of truth for shifts — Publish once. Everyone sees the same roster. Changes are reflected without resending Excel files.
  • Leave out of WhatsApp — Requests, approvals and history stay inside the app. Month-end reconciliation becomes a report, not a detective job.
  • Self-service for operators — “My Shift” and clear status reduce interruptions to the supervisor’s phone.
  • Faster onboarding of new joiners — Add to team + assign section/machine + they can log in the same day.
  • Audit-friendly trail — Approvals and history help when HR or plant head asks for records.

Plants that move from pure Excel + WhatsApp typically report several hours saved per week for the person who owns the roster — time that can go back into process quality, safety, or production follow-up.

💡 Practical tip: Start with one section (e.g. Metalliser A) for two weeks. Once the shift and leave flow is smooth, expand to the full department. Do not try to migrate every historical leave record on day one — start clean and move forward.

1. Man Power वास्तव में क्या करता है

Man Power एक PWA है (फोन और डेस्कटॉप दोनों पर चलता है) जो पैकेजिंग प्लांट के वर्कफोर्स कोऑर्डिनेशन पर फोकस करता है:

  • टीम अपलोड और संरचना — Excel से मेंबर इंपोर्ट (Section / Machine / Responsibility)। मैनेजर अपनी टीम देखते हैं; एडमिन पूरा प्लांट।
  • शिफ्ट शेड्यूलिंग — स्मार्ट फिल्टर के साथ शिफ्ट प्लान बनाएँ और पब्लिश करें। टीम मेंबर “My Shift” खोलकर तुरंत अपनी पोस्टिंग देख लेते हैं।
  • छुट्टी और अटेंडेंस फ्लो — छुट्टी के अनुरोध अप्रूवल से गुजरते हैं, बिखरे WhatsApp मैसेज की जगह। पेंडिंग आइटम बंद होने तक दिखते रहते हैं।
  • रिपोर्ट्स — सुपरवाइज़र और प्लांट हेड के लिए त्वरित व्यू, हर हफ्ते Excel दोबारा बनाने की जरूरत नहीं।
  • To-Do और निर्देश — खुले काम और डिपार्टमेंट निर्देश कैप्चर करें ताकि ज्ञान सिर्फ एक व्यक्ति के दिमाग में न रहे।
  • ट्रेनिंग / Learn मॉड्यूल — स्किल डेवलपमेंट और प्रोसेस नोट्स के लिए जगह।

3. समय कैसे बचता है

  • शिफ्ट का एक सोर्स ऑफ ट्रुथ — एक बार पब्लिश करें। सबको एक ही रोस्टर दिखे।

  • ऑडिट-फ्रेंडली ट्रेल — जब HR या प्लांट हेड रिकॉर्ड माँगे तो हिस्ट्री मौजूद।

Excel + WhatsApp से आने वाले प्लांट आमतौर पर रोस्टर संभालने वाले व्यक्ति के हर हफ्ते कई घंटे बचाते हैं — समय जो प्रोसेस क्वालिटी, सेफ्टी या प्रोडक्शन फॉलो-अप में जा सकता है।
